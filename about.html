Traditional principles, presented for modern clients.

Auric Aura Fengshui was created to bring traditional Chinese Feng Shui principles into contemporary homes, businesses and real-estate decisions.

Our approach respects classical Chinese methods while presenting the analysis in a clear, structured and practical format suitable for modern clients.

Rather than applying one-size-fits-all rules, each consultation considers the individual property, its environment, orientation, timing and — where relevant — the people using the space.

Our Approach

Ancient Wisdom. Modern Spaces. Personal Insight.

Feng Shui is more than interior decoration. Traditional Chinese Feng Shui studies the relationship between people, space, direction, time, and the surrounding environment. Consultations may incorporate classical methods including:

峦头

Luan Tou

Form School. Analysis of the surrounding landscape, buildings, roads, entrances and physical environment.

玄空

Xuan Kong Flying Stars

Analysis of directional and time-based influences within a property.

八宅

Ba Zhai

Eight Mansions. Evaluation of important areas and directions within the home.

八字

BaZi

Four Pillars. Traditional analysis based on an individual’s birth information.

择日

Date Selection

Selection of suitable dates for important activities according to traditional Chinese methods.

Every property and every person is different. Our recommendations are therefore individually prepared rather than generated from a generic checklist.
